- **AI capex remains the master variable.** Hyperscaler spending still drives the highest-quality semiconductor revenues. That supports **VanEck Semiconductor UCITS ETF (SMH.MI)** and **NVIDIA Corporation (1NVDA.MI)** most directly, while **WisdomTree Artificial Intelligence UCITS ETF - USD Acc (WTAI.MI)** captures a broader but less precise version of the same theme.

- **Rates are supportive, not a free pass.** ECB easing and a still-plausible Fed-cut path help long-duration growth assets, but the market has already priced part of that relief. Any shift from “cuts later” to “inflation risk again” would hit AI and semiconductor multiples quickly.

- **EUR dynamics matter for Milan-listed access.** The watchlist is analysed in EUR, and no FX effect was flagged in the supplied data. Still, a firmer euro can soften translated returns for USD-heavy underlying exposures, particularly global AI and semiconductor ETFs with US-dominated holdings.

## Risks & Watchpoints

- **Policy/export risk:** Any tightening of US export controls on advanced AI chips could hit sentiment toward **NVIDIA Corporation (1NVDA.MI)** and spill into **VanEck Semiconductor UCITS ETF (SMH.MI)**.

- **Expectation risk:** AI leaders now need flawless execution. Strong results may not be enough if guidance, margins or supply-chain commentary fail to clear elevated investor expectations.

- **Cyclical recovery risk:** **STMicroelectronics N.V. (STMMI.MI)** has re-rated on recovery confidence, but auto and industrial end-markets still need confirmation through orders, inventory and margin stabilisation.

- **Theme dispersion risk:** **WisdomTree Artificial Intelligence UCITS ETF - USD Acc (WTAI.MI)** may lag if market leadership narrows to hardware infrastructure and away from broader AI adopters.

- **Macro repricing risk:** Higher real yields, tariff-led inflation or a stronger euro could compress valuation multiples and reduce EUR returns for globally exposed instruments.
