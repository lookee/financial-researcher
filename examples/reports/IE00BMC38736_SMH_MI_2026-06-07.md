## What It Is  
The VanEck Semiconductor UCITS ETF seeks to track the performance of the semiconductor industry, offering exposure to companies engaged in the design, distribution, manufacture, and sale of semiconductors. The fund is backed by VanEck Asset Management B.V., which acts as both the issuer and benchmark. The ETF utilizes a full replication methodology, providing investors direct access to the semiconductor market.
